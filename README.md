Employee Attrition Analysis 📊

This project focuses on analyzing employee attrition data to understand the main factors that may lead employees to leave a company.

🎯 Project Objective

The goal is to explore employee data, identify patterns related to attrition, and generate useful insights that can help businesses better understand employee turnover.

🔍 What I Did

- Cleaned and prepared the dataset.
- Checked and handled missing values and data quality issues.
- Performed Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).
- Analyzed the relationship between employee attrition and different factors.
- Created visualizations to clearly present the findings.
- Extracted key business insights from the data.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Plotly
- Jupyter Notebook

📈 Key Areas of Analysis

- Attrition by department
- Attrition by job role
- Attrition by business travel
- Attrition by overtime
- Attrition by age
- Attrition by job satisfaction
- Attrition by income
- Attrition by years at company

💡 Outcome

The analysis provides a clearer understanding of the factors associated with employee attrition and demonstrates how data analysis and visualization can support better HR and business decisions.
